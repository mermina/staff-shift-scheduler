Staff Shift Scheduler

Rota generators that run in the browser. You describe your staff, the cover you need and your rules, and the tool builds the month, checks it, and shows each person's hours. No install and no account; each demo is a single HTML file.

Café & restaurant

Bistró Dæmi, a fictional café-restaurant with 14 staff.

  • Opening, mid and closing shifts; a key-holder on every open and close
  • Wage cost incl. on-costs and labour % of sales
  • Close the month: archive it and carry hour balances forward
  • Hour weights (vægi) and a phone view for staff

Care home (24/7)

A fictional care home with 20 staff and day, evening and night shifts.

  • A night person every night, a supervisor every weekday, one 7:00 opener
  • Fixed patterns kept, everyone else scheduled around them
  • Weighted hours and a balance per person
  • Month archive, saving to a file, Icelandic/English
